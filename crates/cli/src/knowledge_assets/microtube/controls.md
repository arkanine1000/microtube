# Studio Controls

MicroTube is designed to be driven from the keyboard. You can treat it like a
small instrument: choose a parameter, nudge it, listen, and nudge again.

## Movement

- `Tab` switches between Studio and Knowledge.
- `j` and `k` move down and up through the parameter list.
- `h` and `l` make small left and right adjustments.
- `H` and `L` make larger adjustments.
- `q` quits.

In the Knowledge tab, the same `j/k`, `d/u`, and `g/G` habits move through
lists and articles.

## Sound controls

Base changes the pitch of the carrier. It can make the whole sound feel lower,
warmer, thinner, or brighter.

Beat changes the speed of the binaural pulse. This is the number that maps to
Delta, Theta, Alpha, Beta, and Gamma labels.

Volume controls the final output level. Start lower than you think you need.

Warmth controls how much harmonic color is added to the carrier.

Emergence controls the little ecosystem of extra voices.

Gravity controls how strongly Fuxian emergence pulls new voices back toward
the root after motion away from it.

Drift controls the loudness of the endless rising or falling Shepard-Risset
layer. Drift Base controls the bottom frequency of that moving stack.

Noise controls the mist layer.

Timer controls the auto-stop window. It is on by default at 60 minutes, can be
set up to 120 minutes in 5-minute small steps or 10-minute large steps, and
pauses while playback is paused.

## Quick toggles

- `a` toggles the timer.
- `t` cycles timbre: Organ, Flute, Bell, Saw.
- `n` toggles mist on or off.
- `m` cycles the mist texture.
- `e` toggles emergence.
- `g` cycles the emergence spawn mode: Canon, Penrose, Fuxian.
- `r` toggles drift.
- `R` reverses drift direction.
- `v` and `V` cycle the visual modes.

## Presets and sequences

Press `p` for quick presets. These are immediate starting points: Deep Sleep,
Meditation, Relaxation, Focus, Flow State. Local presets saved with `S` appear
after the built-ins in the same menu.

Press `d` in the preset menu to delete a selected local preset. Built-in
presets stay fixed.

Press `s` for sequences. These are timed programs that move settings over
minutes instead of asking you to steer constantly.

Presets are useful when you want one stable mood. Sequences are useful when you
want a journey.

## A good habit

Change one thing at a time. MicroTube can become dense quickly. If you change
beat, warmth, mist, drift, and emergence all at once, you will hear motion but
you may not know which control caused it.
