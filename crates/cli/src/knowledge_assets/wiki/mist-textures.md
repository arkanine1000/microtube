# Mist Textures

The mist layer is a low-amplitude noise field summed beneath the binaural
carrier. Five textures are available, each with a different spectral
slope and perceptual character. Cycle them with `m` in the Studio tab.

## Why colour the noise?

White noise has a flat power spectrum: every frequency contributes
equally. Played at any non-trivial level it is bright and fatiguing —
the high frequencies dominate perception because the ear is most sensitive
there. *Colouring* the noise — biasing its spectrum toward lower or
higher frequencies — produces textures that read as warm, airy, oceanic,
or glassy depending on the slope.

Each of MicroTube's five mist types is generated by a different filter
applied to a base white-noise stream, then mixed at a low level (≤ 30%
in most presets) with the binaural tone.

## The five textures

### Pink — *warm*
Spectrum falls at **3 dB/octave**. Equal energy per octave, which means
each octave has the same perceptual weight. Pink noise sits across the
full audio band evenly and is famously the noise the universe seems to
prefer — natural processes from neural firing to ocean tides have
roughly pink-distributed power spectra. It sits between the darker Brown mist
and the brighter White and Blue textures.

### White — *air*
Flat spectrum. Bright, fizzy, the noise most people associate with the
word "static." Used sparingly, it adds presence without colouring the
tone underneath.

### Brown — *surf*
Spectrum falls at **6 dB/octave** (also called *Brownian* noise). The
high frequencies are heavily attenuated; the result reads as low-frequency
rumble, like distant surf or wind in a chimney. Used in the Earth and
Schumann epochs of the Journey sequence — the planetary scales.

### Blue — *glass*
Spectrum *rises* at 3 dB/octave — the inverse of pink. High-frequency
shimmer, very little body. Used in the Stellar Bells epoch of the Journey
to evoke a starfield: bright, spaced-out points against silence.

### Velvet — *sparks*
Not a coloured-noise filter at all. **Velvet noise** is a sequence of
sparse, randomly-placed unit impulses (positive or negative). Most samples
are zero. Conceived in the speech-synthesis literature, it is
psychoacoustically smooth despite being almost entirely silence — the
brain integrates the sparse impulses into a perceived texture without
the clutter of dense noise. Used in the Microtubule and Strange-Loop
epochs of the Journey, where a coherent quantum lattice should sound
*sparse* rather than *fuzzy*.

## How to use them

The mist is meant to be subtle — set Noise around 10–25%. At higher
levels it competes with the binaural carrier and damages the perceived
beat. Below ~5% it is nearly inaudible but still adds a sense of "air"
to the tone.

For the long-form sequences, mist is automated per epoch. For free-running
sessions, pair the texture with the band:

- **Delta / Theta**: Pink or Brown (warm, low-bias)
- **Alpha**: Pink or Velvet (neutral)
- **Beta / Gamma**: Blue or White (bright, alert)
