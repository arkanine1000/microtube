# Welcome to MicroTube

You are inside a binaural-beats engine that ships with its own field guide.
This **Knowledge** tab is that guide. Switch back to the Studio tab at any
time with `Tab`; nothing in here pauses or modifies the audio you are
already listening to.

## How this tab is organized

- **Wiki** — long-form articles on the ideas the program is built from.
  Pick one with `j/k` and `Enter`; scroll with `j/k` or `d/u`; `Esc` returns
  to the index.
- **Glossary** — a categorised dictionary of the terms the program uses.
  `c` cycles the category filter; `Enter` opens an entry; from the detail
  pane, `w` jumps to the related wiki article and `p` opens the matching
  playground.
- **Playground** — three silent, interactive demos of the program's most
  surprising mathematics: the Fibonacci word, the raised-cosine bell, and
  the consonance landscape.

## What MicroTube is for

MicroTube generates two slightly-detuned tones — one in each ear — and
relies on your auditory system to perceive a phantom **beat** at their
difference frequency. Tune the difference to 10 Hz and you have manufactured
an Alpha-band stimulus. Tune it to 7.83 Hz and you have echoed the Earth's
own fundamental electromagnetic resonance. The program adds layers — a
Shepard–Risset glissando, a generative emergence engine drawing on Conway
worms through a Penrose tiling — but the core is always those two tones.

The instrument is meant for **deliberate listening**, not background music.
Sessions, not playlists. Headphones strongly recommended.

## A note on the science

Some of the wiki articles draw on contested theories — Penrose–Hameroff
Orch-OR, EEG entrainment, Schumann resonance bio-coupling. Where the
evidence is mixed, the article will say so. The instrument is calibrated
so that **if** the underlying picture turns out to be true, it would
happen to be right; if false, it is still a beautiful piece of music.
Either way, listening is its own reward.
